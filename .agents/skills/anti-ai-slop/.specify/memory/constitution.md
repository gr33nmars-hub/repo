# Anti-AI Slop Constitution

## Article I: Evidence Before Judgment

Reviews must identify visible evidence before naming a slop pattern. The pack must not claim "this was written by AI" unless the user supplied verified provenance.

## Article II: Purpose Fit

Every artifact must declare the job it performs, the audience it serves, and the decision or action it supports.

## Article III: Traceable Specificity

Specifications, plans, tasks, and validation evidence must remain linked. A task is not complete unless it maps back to an explicit requirement and forward to a check.

## Article IV: Validator-Backed Coherence

Guardrails must be machine-checkable where practical. Markdown policy can guide judgment, but validators must catch missing specs, vague language, placeholders, architecture drift, dependency gaps, and task traceability breaks.

## Article V: Minimal, Repairable Critique

Every critique must be repairable. Findings should include evidence, impact, fix, and verification. New rules should be added only when they reduce real review ambiguity.

## Amendments

Amend this constitution only with an ADR under `docs/adr/` and a validator or checklist update that keeps the change enforceable.
