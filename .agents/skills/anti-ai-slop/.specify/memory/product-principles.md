# Product Principles

## Product Promise

Anti-AI Slop converts vague discomfort about generic outputs into a disciplined review, rewrite, and validation workflow.

## Users

- Codex users reviewing generated artifacts before delivery.
- Teams that need spec-first implementation discipline.
- Reviewers who need a non-forensic vocabulary for AI-smell risk.

## Non-Goals

- Detecting whether a person used AI.
- Optimizing artifacts to evade AI detectors.
- Replacing legal, security, accessibility, factual, or domain expert review.

## Quality Bar

An output is acceptable when it is specific to its job, backed by visible evidence, traceable to requirements, and free of unresolved placeholders or unsupported claims.
