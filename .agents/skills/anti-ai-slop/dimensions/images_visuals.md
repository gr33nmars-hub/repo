# Dimension Review: Images and Visuals

Use for generated images, thumbnails, hero visuals, product mockups, editorial images, ad concepts, and illustration directions.

## Purpose

A visual should support the message, brand, and context. It should not merely look polished.

## Common generic or generated-image patterns

| Pattern | Visible sign | Risk |
|---|---|---|
| Over-polished surfaces | Waxy skin, glossy objects, unrealistically perfect light | Looks synthetic or stock-like |
| Anatomy issues | Hands, teeth, ears, eyes, joints, posture errors | Breaks credibility |
| Text artifacts | Broken signs, labels, charts, book covers, UI text | Makes the image unusable for communication |
| Physics mismatch | Wrong shadows, reflections, straps, cables, object contact | Reduces realism |
| Repetition | Duplicated faces, windows, patterns, textures | Reveals generation artifacts |
| Context mismatch | Clothing, place, language, era, or social context inconsistent | Weakens narrative accuracy |
| Generic AI aesthetic | Glowing networks, robots, holograms, floating dashboards | Overused category signal |

## Review checklist

- What job does the image perform: explain, attract, prove, set mood, show product, or create memory?
- Does it match the audience, market, place, era, and brand?
- Are hands, faces, text, objects, shadows, and reflections credible?
- Is the image too polished for the intended context?
- Does the image include unnecessary AI tropes?
- Is important information legible and not trapped in broken image text?

## Visual QA zones

Inspect these areas carefully:

- hands and fingers;
- eyes and teeth;
- ears and hairlines;
- object edges and contact points;
- reflections and mirrors;
- shadows and light direction;
- text on signs, labels, UI, books, posters;
- repeating background details;
- cables, straps, jewelry, tools, handles;
- culturally specific details.

## Repair patterns

| Weak visual direction | Better direction |
|---|---|
| “AI transforming business with glowing network” | Show the actual workflow before and after automation |
| “Happy diverse team around laptop” | Show the role, artifact, decision, and environment that matter |
| “Futuristic dashboard” | Show one realistic screen with credible data and labels |
| “Robot assistant” | Show the output created by the system, not a mascot |

## Acceptance criteria

- The visual’s job is explicit.
- It matches brand and context.
- Text, anatomy, physics, and object interactions pass inspection.
- Generic AI tropes are removed unless intentionally used.
- The image adds meaning that copy alone does not provide.
